## PART 1 — The Wedge

### Buyer
Funded startups and growing companies, roughly 20–80 people. Decision-maker is usually the founder/CEO or an exec — empowered, fast, and pedigree-aware. Reached through targeted outbound to recently-funded companies, inbound from your AI point of view, and communities — not old-employer warm intros.

### Positioning (one line)
**We help funded startups and growing companies put AI to work where it actually moves the numbers — built into your product and operations and shipped to production, by builders who ship rather than consultants who present.**

### The differentiation (the wedge-within-the-wedge)
The market is flooded with non-technical "AI consultants" who deliver decks and prompt lists. You deliver **working AI systems wired into a company's real product, workflows, and data, live in production** — built by an engineer who's shipped at Meta, Apple, and LinkedIn. That single distinction — *we build and ship, they present* — is the whole wedge.

### Outcome, not mechanism
Never sell "AI adoption" or "higher AI usage." Sell what the buyer feels: more revenue, a leaner team, faster shipping, work that used to eat hours now handled. AI is the *how*.

### Land-and-expand
AI Opportunity Audit (front door) → build the single highest-leverage AI system → expand into more builds, automations, integrations, and an ongoing AI partner relationship. The audit is the wedge; everything else is the expansion.

## PART 2 — The AI Opportunity Audit (the front door)

### What it is
Before anyone builds anything, you map where AI can genuinely pay off across the company's product and operations, rank the opportunities by impact vs. effort, and give an honest **buy / build / wait** call on each. The client leaves with a prioritized roadmap and a concrete build plan for the highest-leverage opportunity — whether or not they build it with you.

### What the client gets
- A map of where AI can actually move revenue, speed, or cost across their product and operations — specific to them, not generic
- The 1–3 highest-leverage opportunities, scored by impact and effort
- For the top opportunity: a concrete build plan — what you'd build, how it wires into their existing tools and data, and a real cost and timeline
- An honest buy / build / wait call on each opportunity (you don't sell them what they shouldn't build)
- A 30- and 90-day roadmap

### Price
**From $2,500**, scaling with company size and scope (larger engagements run higher). Priced to qualify serious buyers — a bargain-bin audit signals bargain-bin value, and this buyer reads that correctly.
